Consistency Checks & Guidelines:
  • Consistency Check 1: OPS-002 + OPS-008 + OPS-009 must equal OPS-001 (Operating hours + Planned outage + Forced outage = Calendar hours).
  • Consistency Check 2: OPS-006 must equal OPS-003 - OPS-004 + OPS-005 (Auxiliary consumption = Gross generation - Net export + Import).
  • Consistency Check 3: OPS-009 and OPS-012 must reconcile with the totals on sheet '4 Outages'.
  • Consistency Check 4: OPS-026 + OPS-027 divided by OPS-021 must sit near the ash content declared on sheet 2A. A submission that fails these is returned before it enters the peer set.
