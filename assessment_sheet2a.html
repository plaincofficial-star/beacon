Notes & Guidelines:
  • AR = as received (as fired), not dry basis. LHV as-received is the value that drives heat rate; if you hold only HHV dry, enter it in column G and leave F blank - we will convert using your moisture and hydrogen.
  • Ash fusion temperatures (IDT / ST / HT) and the alkali columns (K2O, Na2O, Cl) are what separate a plant with a slagging problem from one without. If you have a recent fuel analysis certificate, these are on it.
  • SiO2 as % of ash is required if ash valorisation (cement substitution, silica extraction) is in scope.
  • LCU = your local currency unit, as declared at FIN-001. Delivered cost includes haulage to the plant gate.
  • Slot IDs F01-F10 are stable: the same slot must be used for the same fuel on sheet '3 Monthly Data'.
